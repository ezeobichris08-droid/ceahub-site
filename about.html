About the brand

A technology brand with a broader vision.

Chivalry EA Hub is being developed as a technology and digital-systems brand — a home for products that can grow independently while sharing one underlying ecosystem.

Identity

Redefining Nobility for the Digital Age.

The brand takes its visual character from ideas of structure, authority, precision and ambition, translated into a contemporary technology identity.

The emblem combines an obsidian architectural form, a silver structural band and a gold central element. The visual language is deliberately distinctive without requiring every product to look the same.

The idea

One foundation. Many expressions.

CEA Hub is intended to make it possible for individual products to have focused identities and experiences without becoming disconnected projects.

For now, the ecosystem is in its early stage. The foundation is being prepared before the first products go live.
